Some questions about our Earth :

What is our planet:Earth?
Earth is the third planet from the Sun, a terrestrial planet composed of rock and metal, and the only known celestial body to support life.
What is Biodiversity
Biodiversity refers to the variety of all forms of life on Earth, including the diversity of species, genes, and ecosystems, which is essential for ecosystem stability and human well-being.
What are the harms to Earth
Earth faces a range of harmful effects primarily due to human activities such as pollution, deforestation, and greenhouse gas emissions, which threaten ecosystems, climate stability, and human health.
