did you Know?

Did you know that the world's largest pizza was made in Rome, Italy in 2012? It measured 1261.65 square meters (13,580.28 square feet) and was named "Ottavia" in homage to the first Roman emperor Octavian Augustus.

Did you know that honey never spoils? Archaeologists have found pots of honey in ancient Egyptian tombs that are over 3000 years old and still perfectly edible.
